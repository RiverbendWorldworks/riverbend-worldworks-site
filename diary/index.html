Development diary

From the workshop

Notes from Iron Vigil's closed beta. Newer entries appear first on our Facebook page.

A guided first mission and team multiplayer

Build 0.6.3 · October 22, 2026

The day after our first tester reports came in, 0.6.3 went out:

  • A guided first mission: the battle pauses while a narrated card explains each step, and you can skip it anytime
  • You start the first mission with enough salvage to build a factory right away
  • A multiplayer lobby for up to 16 players, with three new mirrored team maps so neither side has an edge
  • Press F8 anywhere to send us a bug report or an idea
  • New weapon effects, layered battle sounds, and a music score that rises as the fighting starts
  • Fixed: freezes and stutter, home-network games that wouldn't link, and there's finally a Quit button

Thanks to our testers for the reports that shaped this build.

The same-night hotfix

Build 0.6.2.1 · October 21, 2026

A few hours after 0.6.2 went to testers, a hotfix followed:

  • The Vigil Engine can now build basic units from its rear port, so you're never stuck without a factory
  • The build bar always shows your units, with a tip on what to build first
  • A true fog of war: unexplored ground is black, ground you've explored but can't see is grey
  • Crews about to break shout "Breaking!", and routing crews complain loudly ("I don't get paid enough for this!")
  • Fixed: the campaign radio was silent because most missions had no calls written. Every mission now has radio chatter
