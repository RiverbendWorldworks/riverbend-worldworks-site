Games

Our games

An original strategy game, an adaptation in development, and an early concept.

Iron Vigil

Closed beta

Iron Vigil is a real-time strategy game of great war machines, where the Vigil and the Dominion fight across a campaign map, in skirmishes and in team multiplayer. It's in closed beta testing with a small group of testers, and we share progress in the development diary.

Last World

In development

Last World is based on the Last World trilogy by Shane Gries: fleet battles in space and fights on the ground, from battleships and carriers to powered armor.

The Thin Dead Line

Concept

An early concept based on Shane Gries's The Thin Dead Line and the Line series. These images are concept work, not an announced game.
