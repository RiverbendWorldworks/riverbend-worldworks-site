The studio

Riverbend Worldworks is a book-to-game studio in Bettendorf, Iowa. We adapt books into games in partnership with their authors and publishers, and we build original games of our own.

Our philosophy

By keeping the cost of making games low with AI and automation, we can give the authors and artists who created a world a larger share of what its game earns, and give readers who love their books another way to experience the same worlds.

We sell our games on Steam, and each game and its books and audiobooks point fans toward one another, so readers find the game and players find the books.
