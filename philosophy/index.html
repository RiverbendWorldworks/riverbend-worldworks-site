Philosophy & business model

Why we make games this way

By keeping the cost of making games low with AI and automation, we can give the authors and artists who created a world a larger share of what its game earns, and give readers who love their books another way to experience the same worlds.

Lower cost, larger share

Games are expensive to make, and that cost limits what is left for the people who created the original story. We use AI and automation throughout development to keep that cost down.

Because the game costs less to make, more of what it earns can go to the authors and artists who created the world. Every partnership's terms are agreed for that project, in writing, before development starts.

Another way into the worlds readers love

Readers who love a series want more time in its world. A game lets them command its fleets, defend its cities and make its hardest choices themselves, in a world they already care about.

How the business works

  • We adapt books into games in partnership with their authors and publishers. The story stays with the people who hold its rights.
  • We sell the games on Steam.
  • The games and the books promote each other. Players discover the books and audiobooks behind the game, and readers discover the game.
  • Authors share in the game's proceeds under terms agreed for each project.
  • We also build original games and free Minecraft mods.

The company

Riverbend Worldworks LLC is an independent studio in Bettendorf, Iowa.
