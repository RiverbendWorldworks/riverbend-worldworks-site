FAQ

Questions

Short answers for players, readers and authors.

What does Riverbend Worldworks make?

We adapt books into games with their authors and publishers, build original games, and make free Minecraft mods.

Can I play Iron Vigil?

Iron Vigil is in closed beta with a small group of testers. There's no public release date, demo or store page yet. Follow us on Facebook, Instagram or YouTube for news.

Where will the games be sold?

On Steam. We'll link a store page here once one is live.

Do you use AI?

Yes. We use AI and automation in development to keep costs down, which is how we can offer authors a larger share. People at the studio direct and review the work. Read our philosophy.

Who owns the story?

The story stays with its author or rights holder. Each adaptation runs under its own written agreement.

I'm an author. How do I start?

Email us with your book or series, the author or publisher, the rights status and a few links. See Work with us.

How do I report a bug in a mod?

Use the issue tracker on the mod's CurseForge page, or email riverbendworldbuilders@gmail.com.
