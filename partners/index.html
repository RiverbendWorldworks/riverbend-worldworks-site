For authors & publishers

Work with us

We turn books into games together with the people who wrote and publish them.

Email us about your book

1. Start with an email

Write to riverbendworldbuilders@gmail.com with the subject "Book-to-game partnership" and tell us:

  • the book or series
  • the author, and the publisher if there is one
  • the rights status: who holds the game or interactive rights, and whether anyone else needs to agree
  • links to the book, the author's site or reviews

Please don't send an unpublished manuscript, artwork or audio until we've agreed in writing how that material will be handled.

2. Find the right game

Not every story is a strategy game. We talk through what kind of game fits your world, from strategy and tactics to role-playing and adventure, before anything is decided.

3. Agree the terms in writing

Before development starts, a written agreement sets out the game, the platforms, the rights involved, creative approvals, and your share of the game's proceeds. The story stays yours. Every agreement deserves your own professional review.

4. Build together

You see the work as it develops, with agreed review points for the decisions that matter to you. When the game launches on Steam, the game and your books and audiobooks promote each other.

Why partner with us

By keeping the cost of making games low with AI and automation, we can give the authors and artists who created a world a larger share of what its game earns, and give readers who love their books another way to experience the same worlds. Read our philosophy.
